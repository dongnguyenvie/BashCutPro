## How this run was made

- **Machine and build**: mac 02 (Apple silicon, macOS 15), BashCutPro `main` 7cc00504, dev build of `filmcraft-cli`
  and `bashcut-batch` (default features: no Whisper).
- **Inputs**: `resources/raw/` is not on this machine, so the clips were generated (`target/fixtures/bench/`, never
  committed): a 17 s talking head (macOS `say` voice over a test pattern, 1280x720), three 5 s b-roll patterns and a
  32 s kick + tone bed (120 BPM). No Whisper in the build: the transcript is the known script with even word times
  (`transcript.set`), so the edit / caption timing numbers measure the commands, not a transcriber.
- **Runs**: each job is one `bashcut-batch run` on a prepared `.fcproj` (media placed, transcript set) with command
  steps that follow the job's skills, exported with the job's output profile:
  - talking-head-short: fill 9:16 (Motion scale), `transcript.removeFillers` + `removePauses`, captions
    (`transcript.createCaptions`, `captions.setStyle`, SRT, `bashcut.captions.wordStyle` pop), review, `tiktok`.
  - broll-montage: `clip.fitToFrame`, `bashcut.audio.beats` (markers), `bashcut.looks.apply` teal-orange, Cross
    Dissolves, review, `youtube`.
  - vietnamese-subtitles: translate-captions `apply` as a plugin step with the agent's translation (hand-off, no
    key), caption style, review, `youtube`.
- **Measured** (kit `bench/score.py` format): loudness of the rendered file's audio with the kit's `loudness.py`
  (decoded with ffmpeg as an outside oracle), `bashcut.review.run` on the item project, `bashcut.review.pictureChecks`
  on the rendered file re-imported, cuts from `sequence_inspect` against `transcript.inspect` words, length with
  ffprobe, render time from the job book.
