## Where Pro loses

Nowhere on the scores. Two problems found in this round have `quality` issues:

- [#219](https://github.com/dongnguyenvie/BashCutPro/issues/219): **the tutorial needs about 150 agent turns.** At the pipeline's `maxTurns: 80` the first attempt
  hit the cap while making captions (its last step). The item failed and nothing was exported: 13 min and $4.91
  spent for no output. Of its 81 turns, 34 were single `render_frame` checks and 14 were repeated `command_list`
  lookups.
- [#220](https://github.com/dongnguyenvie/BashCutPro/issues/220) (`owner`): **the kit's `fetch_audio.py` searches music with no licence filter.** The Pro vlog agent
  used a CC BY-NC-ND track, the same one BashCut picked in round 1. That is fine for a bench but not for a published
  vlog.

## How it was run

- **Builds.**
  - Pro: debug builds (dev profile) of BashCutPro `main` at 330ee6f1, with `filmcraft-cli` built with `whisper`.
  - Kit: `bashcutpro-agent-kit` at b242ff8 (plugin `bcp` 0.2.21).
  - This includes every fix listed above and kit PRs #43–#54.
- **Agent.**
  - Pro used `bashcut-batch run <pipeline> --kit <bashcutpro-agent-kit>` with the batch's **built-in** claude agent ([#208](https://github.com/dongnguyenvie/BashCutPro/pull/208)). Round 1 used a wrapper script; this round's agent steps run as the product runs them. The kit is a `--plugin-dir` with the kit's tools, the model is `claude-opus-5-5`, and every step has `fixRounds: 2` and the review gate.
  - Briefs, footage and output profiles are the same as round 1.
  - Two jobs ran at a time, so render times are not comparable with round 1 (not scored either way).
- **Turn cap.**
  - Round 1's wrapper did not stop the agent at `maxTurns: 80`: the vlog used 172 turns and the tutorial 137. The built-in agent stops at the cap and fails the item.
  - The tutorial's first attempt failed that way ([#219](https://github.com/dongnguyenvie/BashCutPro/issues/219)). It was re-run with `maxTurns: 160`, about what round 1 effectively allowed, and finished in 2 rounds and 150 turns. That second attempt is what is scored.
  - The other five jobs finished under 80.
- **Scoring:** the kit's `score.py`, measured the same way as round 1 (see [round 1](2026-10-20-real-footage.md#how-it-was-run)). The review category still uses each side's own reviewer.
