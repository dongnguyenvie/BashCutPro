# Library: your pieces and open media online

**Library** on the rail has two tabs. Both need a plugin: **Online** the *Online Resources* plugin, **Mine** the
*Library* plugin (install them in **Plugins**; the tab tells you when one is missing and **Open Plugins** takes you
there).

## Online

Find music, sound effects, photos, video, icons and fonts that you are allowed to use, without leaving the app.

1. Pick a kind: **Music**, **Sound effects**, **Photos**, **Video**, **Icons**, **Fonts**.
2. Type what you need ("calm acoustic", "whoosh", "rice field drone") and **Search**. Nothing is downloaded yet.
3. Each result shows its title, author, length or size and **its licence** before you take it. *Licence unknown:
   check before publishing* in red means just that.
4. **Preview** opens the file in your browser; **Source** opens the page it comes from.
5. **Place at playhead** downloads it and puts it on the timeline at the playhead, on a new track (nothing you made
   is covered). **Add to bin** only brings it into the project, where you can preview it in the Source monitor
   first. Fonts are downloaded only: install the font in your system to use it in titles.

Every downloaded file keeps a licence record next to it (`<file>.licence.json`: licence, author, source, date).
Searches run in the background (see **Runs**) and recent results are remembered.

**Credits are your choice.** With **Credit lines in the project notes** off (the default), nothing about licences
goes into your project. Turn it on when you will publish credits: each file you place adds one line to a credits
block in the project notes (**Window ▸ Project Notes**), without repeats.

Sources: Openverse, Freesound (with a free key in the plugin's settings), Wikimedia Commons, the Internet Archive,
Pixabay and Pexels (with their free keys), Iconify, Google Fonts / Fontsource.

## Mine

Your own reusable pieces, saved by the Library plugin: titles and text styles, stickers and overlays, sounds and
music beds, looks and LUTs, effect presets, project templates and brand kits.

Search them by words and kind. ★ marks favourites. **Place at playhead** puts a sound, music bed, sticker or overlay
on the timeline; **Apply** uses the other kinds (a title, a look, an effect preset…) through the plugin, which needs
the app's control channel ([Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)).
